## Normalized boundaries

- Canonical, historical and experimental information are states within one
  governed knowledge architecture, not three competing source-of-truth systems.
- An exact or semantic duplicate remains visible in provenance while mapping to
  one canonical concept ID.
- “Blockchain-backed” is not synonymous with integrity, identity, backup or
  recovery. Approved cryptography and tested storage semantics remain separate.
- “Quantum-safe” is a qualification claim requiring an approved algorithm,
  provider, parameter set, migration plan and test evidence.
- A community hardware profile is declared evidence, not observed compatibility.
- A model, framework, protocol, language or provider name is research metadata;
  Genesis adopts mechanisms through stable provider-neutral contracts.
- A generated robot, application, infrastructure plan or policy is a candidate,
  not an executed action.
- Historical “healing” and frequency material cannot become diagnosis, treatment
  or efficacy claims without appropriate scientific and regulatory evidence.
- Local-first means useful bounded operation without a network dependency; it
  does not mean all capabilities are present locally.
- Parent/child software releases are not biological parents or descendants.
- Digital-twin, whole-brain, consciousness and subjective-state language does
  not prove equivalence or conscious experience.

## Honest integration status

The source is preserved, occurrence-indexed, internally deduplicated and mapped
to stable requirement families. This does not implement the 100-family protocol
catalogue, historical BITNET plan, platform suite, robotics stack or compiled
vision. New requirements begin as `SPECIFIED`; research-only material remains
evidence-gated. Subsequent increments must create concrete interfaces, behavior,
tests, benchmarks, security, recovery and platform evidence before any family can
advance through the Genesis completion gates.
