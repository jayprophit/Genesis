Identical content.

Earlier versions of a concept subsequently improved.

It describes an architecture closer to a:

**Constitutionally governed artificial-intelligence research ecosystem + scientific discovery engine + multi-agent system + digital-twin platform + biological signal intelligence system + simulation laboratory + predictive engine + neuromorphic research environment + quantum simulation platform + environmental guardian.**

> **Develop knowledge and capability while promoting peace, love, harmony, respect, human wellbeing, scientific truth and ecological balance, and while minimizing unnecessary harm and irreversible destruction.**

That gives you a much stronger foundation for the Genesis program than simply encoding a collection of "AI laws."
