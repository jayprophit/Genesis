# Architectural boundaries

- **Genome:** inheritable construction and development information.
- **Identity:** continuity of one individual existence.
- **Lineage:** ancestry and divergence relationships.
- **Memory:** accumulated or inherited information carrying an origin label.
- **RNA-like expression:** temporary instructions derived from genome, never an implicit mutation.
- **Current state:** present runtime conditions, never silently inherited.

The three-strand design is an engineered digital analogy, not a claim about human DNA. Chemistry, cells, tissues, organs, sleep, affect and immune terminology remain architectural metaphors until falsifiable implementations show measured value.
